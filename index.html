About Me

Building reliable full-stack systems with strong foundations in computer science and modern web frameworks.

I am a third-year Computer Science and Engineering student at KCC Institute of Technology and Management, Greater Noida (Class of 2023–2027). I focus on engineering web applications that are dependable, performant, and maintainable from the database layer to the browser.

My hands-on experience centers on the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js). Rather than just assembling frontend templates, I build full lifecycle features: architecting RESTful APIs, securing routes with JWT and Bcrypt, modeling database schemas, and crafting responsive user interfaces with React and Tailwind CSS.

I anchor my software development in core computer science disciplines — Data Structures & Algorithms, Object-Oriented Programming, Database Management Systems, Operating Systems, and Computer Networks. Having this foundation helps me write structured code, design efficient queries, and reason clearly about concurrency and performance.

Right now, I am actively sharpening my Python proficiency and exploring Generative AI architectures. My goal is to combine robust full-stack engineering with modern AI capabilities to build intelligent, high-impact software.
